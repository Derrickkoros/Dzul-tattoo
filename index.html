More than a tattoo

A mark with a meaning.

Every person walks in carrying a story. A memory. A turning point. A name they never want to forget, or a version of themselves they fought hard to become.

At Dzul Ink Lounge, ink is more than an image on skin. It is a way to hold on, let go, begin again, or simply make something beautiful that is entirely your own.

“The most personal art doesn't need to explain itself. You know what it means.”

The people behind the ink

Three artists.
Infinite stories.

Different hands, one shared respect for the trust it takes to turn something personal into something permanent.

ARTIST / 01

Andrew Smith

Andrew grew up watching his father’s sketchbooks fill with quiet symbols — dates, initials, little icons that held whole chapters of a life. He learned early that the most powerful images are rarely the loudest. In the chair, he listens longer than he draws, translating the things people struggle to say into patient, thoughtful work that feels unmistakably theirs.

Thoughtful detail · Personal symbolism · Black & grey
ARTIST / 02

Lucy Davis

Lucy found her way to ink after years of painting the delicate things people often overlook — the curve of a petal, the weight of a single word. She believes beauty can be soft and still carry immense strength. Her pieces invite people to wear the quiet parts of themselves with pride, turning vulnerability into something lasting and luminous.

Fine line · Floral & botanical · Soft color
ARTIST / 03

Mark Ford

Mark’s path to tattooing began the day he decided the chapters that almost broke him deserved to be worn, not hidden. Bold composition and strong imagery became his language for courage. He works with people who are ready to be seen — turning turning points, losses, and victories into work that feels like it has always belonged on their skin.

Bold imagery · Large-scale · Strong composition
